Live site: https://monikaspices.netlify.app

How to put this site on Netlify (free)

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
3. Drag this whole folder onto the page (the folder that contains index.html).
4. Netlify will publish the site and give you a link.

No build step. No Next.js. This is a normal HTML/CSS/JS website.
